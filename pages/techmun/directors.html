Aditi Kodukula

Under Secretary-General

Hey everyone! Welcome to TechMUN XXXIII! My name is Aditi Kodukula, and I’ll be serving as your Undersecretary-General for this year’s conference. TechMUN has been an integral part of my life for the last six years. Whether it was as a middle school delegate in an Atlantis committee, as a dancing panda while directing the 2024 JCC, or as a candygram messenger last April, TechMUN has shaped my experiences as both a competitor and leader – and I couldn’t be more grateful. As cliche as it sounds, I’ve grown up through this club and conference, and can’t wait to see the impact it has on all of you. I encourage you to take risks, write that JPD, run that merger – because if not at TechMUN, where else?
Outside of MUN, you can find me dancing, blasting music in my room, retrieving stolen clothes from my sister, or [attempting to] parallel park (thanks Mohan!) I’m guilty of stalking the Crumbl website every week to judge the flavor selection, but never end up buying anything. Thanks to Abhiram and Ryan, I also started watching Stranger Things this year – and was very disappointed when conformity gate did not happen. If you’re in the Stranger Things or Mahabharata committees, I’ll be especially eager to see how your arcs flow!
After hours of planning and preparation, we’re so excited to present our 33rd iteration of the conference to you in the coming weeks. Should you ever need anything, feel free to contact Secretariat at tjmodelun@gmail.com, or me at aditi.kodukula@gmail.com. See you in March!

Mohan Parthasarathy

Director of General Staff

Hi everyone! My name is Mohan Parthasarathy, and I’m so excited to serve as your Director of General Staff for TechMUN this year. For the last several months, the rest of Secretariat and I have worked tirelessly in order to bring this conference together. Working with every chair, director, member of staff, and alum, as well as our incredible sponsor Dr. Ross, has undoubtedly been one of the most fun and exciting experiences of my high school career. From figuring out dias for each committee at 2AM to laughing uncontrollably during each officer meeting with Arjun, the journey leading up to TechMUN is one that I won’t forget. As a junior struggling to get through classes every week, this club and conference have given me some of the best, most fun experiences of my life.
Outside of MUN, you’ll probably find me eating, watching Netflix in my room - my favorite show is Brooklyn Nine-Nine, and I’ve watched it 100 times (ask me anything about it). Right now, I’m watching Modern Family, which a certain one of your other directors (who happens to have awful taste in shows, and music, too) recommended. Other than TV, I love to blast Drake in the car and sing my heart out while I drive around my friends (or teach Aditi to parallel park). You also might see me complaining about being bored of wearing a trench coat to committee, but doing it anyway.
Whether you walk out of the weekend with a gavel or a gloomy Instagram note, I want TechMUN to be the conference you all leave most proud of because of the memories and friends you made during debate. Step out of your comfort zones, try something new, let your creativity run wild (crisis delegates - I am talking to you), and most importantly, make fun of the rest of Sec if you see them. But don’t tell them I said that. With all that said, feel free to contact me with any questions at tjmodelun@gmail.com (or personally at mohanjairam2018@gmail.com). I look forward to seeing all of you this March and I wish all of you the best of luck!

Zoeb Izzi

Director of Technology

Goooood morning delegates! My name is Zoeb Izzi and I’m honored to serve as your Director of Technology for TechMUN XXXIII. This is my fifth year doing MUN, and ever since I joined, I’ve loved every second of it, from debating interstellar diplomacy in a Star Trek committee to channeling my Nymphadora Tonks to create a dragon army. From running around the streets of Williamsburg and Philadelphia, to planning opening speeches while I’m walking up to speak, to navigating leading a 75 delegation bloc while my partner “accidentally” slept in, MUN never fails to keep me on my toes. Outside of MUN, you’ll find me watching TwoSet Violin, filling up entire whiteboards with physics problems, or engineering new ways to make things work (and, in the process, breaking 2 things that used to work).
To all the delegates joining us for TechMUN this year, my biggest advice is to have fun. Sometimes mergers can be stressful or updates might not go your way, but the most important part of any conference are the memories you take with you, a gift better than any award. Whether this is your first conference or your last, be sure to make the most of it.
As your Director of Technology, I’ll be ensuring that TechMUN runs smoothly and that this year’s conference is our best yet. Please feel free to reach out to zoeb.m.izzi@gmail.com if you have any questions. I can’t wait to see you all in March, and good luck delegates!
