Arjun Garg

Director of Crisis Simulations

Hi everyone! My name is Arjun Garg, and I’m a junior serving as your Director of Crisis for TechMUN XXXIII. This is my fourth year in Model UN and my third TechMUN, and over the years I’ve discovered one undeniable truth: nothing beats the chaos of a well-run crisis committee. From plotting elaborate arcs to watching delegates pull off absolute masterclasses in strategy (or complete disasters that somehow still work), crisis has easily been the most exciting part of my MUN experience.
Over the years, MUN has given me some of my favorite memories in high school: late-night conference prep, ridiculous arcs that somehow worked, and moments where creativity beat preparation (and vice versa). Crisis, especially, has taught me how to think on my feet, adapt under pressure, and commit fully to an idea, even when it feels a little insane. Those are the same qualities I want to see from every delegate this year. As Director of Crisis, my goal is to make every committee feel fast-paced, immersive, and unforgettable. I’m looking for delegates who are willing to take risks, think creatively, and commit fully to their roles, whether that means orchestrating a brilliant long-term plan or improvising when everything goes wrong (which it will). Being good at a crisis committee isn’t about doing the most; it’s about doing the smartest thing at the right time, and doing it with confidence and diplomacy.
Outside of MUN, you’ll probably find me experimenting with new recipes (although I always enjoy a nice restaurant), listening to Drake, running, binge-watching action-thrillers (I absolutely love the Mission: Impossible franchise), or being concerned by Mohan’s driving. Planning TechMUN alongside the rest of Secretariat has been one of the most rewarding parts of my high school experience, and I couldn’t be more excited to share that with all of you. I hope TechMUN 2027 is the conference where you push yourself, surprise yourself, and walk away with memories you’ll talk about long after awards are handed out. If you have any questions about crisis committees, TechMUN, or TJ in general, feel free to reach out at arjungarghd@gmail.com. Can’t wait to see you all on April 2-3, 2027!
