Aashay Mody

Guest Speaker

Aashay Mody is an undergraduate student at Cornell University, majoring in Environment and Sustainability and developing experience in the field of environmental finance. Alongside pursuing these learning opportunities, he is involved with numerous environmental organizations, including Climate Cardinals, where he serves as Deputy Director for Climate Education and helps to lead the Climate Ambassadors Program. Additionally, Aashay is a published author, Global Sustainability Challenge Partner Challenge Award Winner, SDG Design Challenge Winner, U.S. Department of Energy AlgaePrize Competition finalist, and Climate Reality Leader.
