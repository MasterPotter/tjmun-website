Why sponsor us

Your support reaches students who care about public service.

TJMUN gives students space to practice public speaking, negotiation, research, and policy writing. Sponsors help us keep TechMUN strong while making Model UN more accessible to delegates who need financial help.

Support funds visible conference sponsorships and helps us keep TechMUN strong for students, advisors, and guests throughout the weekend.
