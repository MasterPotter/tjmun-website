Director What’s up warriors!
I’m Arjun, a junior at TJ, and I’m beyond excited to be your director for this JCC. This is my fourth year doing MUN and my third TechMUN, and I can confidently say that nothing matches the intensity, drama, and sheer unpredictability of a crisis committee. And this one will without a doubt be unmatched.
The Mahabharata is more than a story; it’s a battlefield of politics, strategy, loyalty, and ambition. In this committee, you’ll be stepping into the shoes of kings, warriors, and advisors, making decisions that could alter the course of history itself. I want you to embrace the chaos, think creatively, and definitely not be afraid to take risks. Your arcs can and should be bold, dramatic, and full of intrigue. So plot your strategies, form alliances, and be ready for unexpected twists.
When I’m not working on MUN, you’ll probably find me experimenting new recipes at home, listening to Drake, running, wondering how Mohan is allowed to drive, or binge-watching thriller movies. I want to bring that same energy to this committee: fast-paced, intense, and a little unpredictable. My goal is for you to leave this committee not just having survived a war of epic proportions, but having grown as a strategist, negotiator, and storyteller. I want you to push the limits of what you think is possible in a crisis and think outside the box.
Whether you’re a first-timer or a seasoned delegate, I’m here to guide you, challenge you, and make sure that your experience is unforgettable. If you have any questions, feel free to contact me at jccpandavastechmun2026@gmail.com or arjungarghd@gmail.com. Get ready to make history, and I can’t wait to see you all there!
